The home page follows the supplied layout and uses replaceable sample photography. No exact image matching is required. All three banners are editable through Admin > Banners, including separate mobile images, text, buttons, links, visibility, and order.

Existing artwork created before the sample-image clarification:

- summer-collection.webp: sage background with green dress model on the left and clear right space.
- premium-basics.webp: blush background with folded neutral knitwear on the right and clear left space.

These are illustrative images, not actual product photographs. Original PNG copies remain alongside the web images. Live store data is not changed; the demonstration banners are in data/preview.sqlite.
